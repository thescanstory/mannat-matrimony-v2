3. Purpose & Target Audience

Mannat: Elite Matrimony is a private matrimonial matchmaking and family alliance network designed exclusively for marriage-seeking adults (18+) and their families. It provides discreet introductions, BlurShield™ photo privacy protection, and verified biodata exchanges.

4. External Services & Backend Infrastructure

  • Authentication: Sign in with Apple (@capgo/capacitor-social-login), Google Sign-In, and Supabase Auth.
  • Backend & Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL database for real-time messaging, connections, and privacy settings).
  • Storage: Supabase Storage (Encrypted bucket for candidate bio-data & photo uploads).
  • Device APIs: Native Capacitor iOS plugins (Haptics, Status Bar, Splash Screen, Share).
  • Payments / In-App Purchases: None active in this build (all features free to access, no gated consumables or StoreKit subscriptions).
  • Matchmaking Engine: Client-side algorithmic profile compatibility scoring.

5. Compliance, Safety & Regulations

  • Safety & Moderation (Guideline 1.2): Users can report suspicious bio-datas and block candidates with instant removal from feed and connections.
  • Account Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1): Users can permanently wipe their account, bio-data, messages, and uploaded photos anytime under Profile → Delete Account & Data.
  • Regional Differences: None. The app functions identically across all regions.
  • Regulated Industry: Not applicable. Mannat is a matrimonial matchmaking platform and does not operate in regulated domains or use licensed third-party media.
