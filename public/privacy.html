3. BlurShield™ & Discretion Controls

Your photos and detailed dossier remain blurred by default. No member can view unblurred photographs without your explicit mutual authorization.

4. In-App Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

You have the absolute right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app:

  1. Navigate to Profile → Settings (Privacy Lock).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Delete Account & Wipe Data”.
  3. Confirm deletion. All bio-datas, photos, chats, and authentication records are permanently purged immediately.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest via AES-256 hosted on SOC-2 certified cloud infrastructure.
